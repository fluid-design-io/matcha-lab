# Matcha Lab — Drink Content Research

Research for a design-led Japanese-inspired digital café menu. 16 candidates gathered, 9 recommended.

## The shared matcha base

Every drink on this menu is built from **one** preparation:

> **Matcha base** — sift **3 g** matcha into a chawan (or a wide mug). Add **35–40 ml water at 75–80 °C**. Whisk briskly with a chasen in a W-motion for ~20 s until smooth with a fine foam. Use immediately.

Every recipe below is then **matcha base + N other ingredients**, N = 0–4.

### A note on the base ratio vs. published sources

Traditional usucha is a *drink*, not a *shot*. Ippodo specifies **2 g matcha / 60 ml water at 80 °C, whisked 15 s** ([Ippodo, Basic Usucha](https://global.ippodo-tea.co.jp/blogs/tea-recipe/basic-usucha-matcha)). Just One Cookbook's latte uses **1½ tsp matcha / 2 Tbsp (30 ml) water at 80 °C** ([JOC](https://www.justonecookbook.com/green-tea-latte/)) — a concentrate, because it gets diluted by milk.

The 3 g / 35–40 ml house base sits deliberately on the concentrate side so it survives milk, ice, and soda. **The one place this matters is the Usucha entry**, where the base is topped up with plain hot water to reach a proper 60 ml bowl. Flagging this rather than pretending 3 g / 37 ml is a ceremonial ratio — it isn't.

Water temperature is the one non-negotiable across every source consulted: **75–80 °C, never boiling**. Matcha will not disperse in cold water, and boiling water makes it bitter.

## Per-drink detail

### 1. Usucha — 薄茶 "thin tea"

**Also:** Classic Matcha, Ceremonial Matcha.
**Provenance: genuinely traditional.** This is the actual, established preparation — the everyday form of matcha in Japanese tea practice, as distinct from *koicha* (thick tea). Ippodo (Kyoto, founded 1717) publishes it as their baseline method.
Source: [Ippodo Tea — Basic Usucha](https://global.ippodo-tea.co.jp/blogs/tea-recipe/basic-usucha-matcha) · [Ippodo US — Usucha (Thin Matcha)](https://ippodotea.com/blogs/recipes-to-brew-japanese-tea/usucha-thin-matcha)

**Recipe (hot, one bowl):**
- Matcha base (3 g / 35–40 ml)
- + 20–25 ml more water at 80 °C, to bring the bowl to ~60 ml total

**Steps:** sift matcha · pour 80 °C water · whisk 15 s in a W · top up and serve in the bowl.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 2 · energy 8 · fresh 7 · level 7
**Note:** Sea-spray bitterness that turns sweet on the swallow; nothing hides here.
**Caveat:** N = 0. It's the reference point of the menu rather than a "recipe", and it needs the top-up to be honest to the traditional 2 g / 60 ml ratio. Ippodo's tip: if you have no thermometer, let boiled water stand ~5 minutes to reach ~80 °C.

### 2. Matcha Latte — 抹茶ラテ (hot)

**Provenance: modern.** Wikipedia is explicit that the matcha latte "is a Western adaptation that emerged in cafés in the late 20th and early 21st centuries," with Starbucks introducing its version in 2006. Japanese *matcha au lait* (抹茶オレ) exists as a café and canned-drink category, but I found no source dating it, so I won't claim antiquity for it.
Source: [Just One Cookbook — Matcha Latte 抹茶ラテ](https://www.justonecookbook.com/green-tea-latte/) · [Wikipedia — Matcha latte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matcha_latte)

**Recipe (hot):**
- Matcha base
- 180 ml milk (dairy, or oat for a sweeter body)
- 1–2 tsp honey or maple (optional; JOC's author skips it)

**Steps:** whisk matcha base in the mug · heat milk to ~65 °C and froth · pour milk through the foam · dust with matcha.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 8 · energy 5 · fresh 2 · level 6
**Note:** A warm green blanket — the grassy edge folded into steamed milk until it purrs.

---

### 3. Iced Matcha Latte — アイス抹茶ラテ

**Provenance: modern.** Same lineage as #2.
Source: [Just One Cookbook — Iced Green Tea Latte](https://www.justonecookbook.com/iced-green-tea-latte/) · [Sudachi — Iced Matcha Latte](https://sudachirecipes.com/iced-matcha-latte/)

**Recipe (iced):**
- Matcha base
- 180 ml cold milk
- 15 ml simple syrup
- 1 cup ice (~150 g)

**Steps:** fill glass with ice · add milk and syrup · whisk matcha base · pour matcha over the back of a spoon · stir before drinking.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 7 · energy 6 · fresh 5 · level 5
**Note:** Green ink dropped into cold milk, blooming as you watch.

---

### 4. Matcha Shakerato

**Provenance: modern, Italian-derived.** The *caffè shakerato* is a real Italian summer drink — espresso and syrup shaken hard with ice to raise a foam head. The matcha version transplants the technique. Not traditional in Japan, but note that Ippodo itself publishes a "Shaken Matcha" (1 g matcha, 100 ml ice water, shaken in a flask), so the shake method has vendor blessing even if the shakerato framing is Italian.
Source: [Ippodo Tea — Shaken Matcha](https://global.ippodo-tea.co.jp/blogs/tea-recipe/shaken-matcha) · [Wikipedia — Shakerato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shakerato) · [Cider & Rye — Matcha Shakerato](http://ciderandrye.com/blog/2015/4/27/matcha-shakerato)

**Recipe (iced, dairy-free):**
- Matcha base
- 100 ml cold water (or coconut water)
- 10–15 ml maple syrup or honey
- 5–6 large ice cubes

**Steps:** whisk matcha base · tip into a jar with ice, water and syrup · shake hard 15 s · strain into a chilled glass, foam last.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 3 · energy 8 · fresh 8 · level 6
**Note:** All the body of a latte and none of the milk — a jade espresso with a meringue collar.

### 5. Matcha Tonic

**Provenance: modern.** Stu's Kitchen is direct about it: the drink "took off after the espresso tonic trend did." No traditional claim from any source I found.
Source: [Nio Teas — Matcha Tonic](https://nioteas.com/blogs/japanese-teas/matcha-tonic-your-new-favorite-summer-mocktail) · [Stu's Kitchen — Matcha Tonic](https://www.stuskitchen.com/blogs/journal/matcha-tonic)

**Recipe (iced, clear):**
- Matcha base
- 150–180 ml chilled tonic water
- Juice of ¼ lime (~7 ml)
- 4–6 large ice cubes

**Steps:** fill glass with ice · pour tonic · squeeze lime into the matcha base · float matcha slowly down the inside of the glass · serve unstirred.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 0 · energy 6 · fresh 9 · level 4
**Note:** Quinine bitterness and matcha bitterness shaking hands over a bed of lime.
**Menu note:** the most photogenic drink in the set — a green disc suspended over clear fizz. Nio Teas' version uses 2 tsp / 200 ml, which is far too dilute to float; the concentrated house base is what makes the layer hold.

### 6. Yuzu Matcha Soda — 柚子抹茶ソーダ

**Provenance: modern.** Yuzu is deeply traditional as an ingredient; this soda format is a contemporary café drink. Nio Teas offers no historical claim.
Source: [Nio Teas — Yuzu Matcha Soda](https://nioteas.com/blogs/japanese-teas/yuzu-matcha) · [Takes Two Eggs — Yuzu Matcha Tonic](https://takestwoeggs.com/yuzu-matcha-tonic/)

**Recipe (iced, sparkling):**
- Matcha base
- 2 Tbsp (~30 g) yuzu jam / *yuzu-cha* marmalade — an Asian-grocery staple; bottled yuzu juice 15–20 ml + 10 ml honey also works
- 200 ml chilled sparkling water
- ½ cup ice

**Steps:** spoon yuzu jam into a tall glass · half-fill with ice · pour sparkling water slowly and stir to dissolve the jam · float matcha on top.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 0 · energy 5 · fresh 10 · level 4
**Note:** Citrus peel and cold rain — the brightest thing on the menu.

---

### 7. Ichigo Matcha Latte — 苺抹茶ラテ (Strawberry)

**Provenance: modern Japanese café trend.** Just One Cookbook calls it a modern café trend explicitly; no traditional lineage claimed.
Source: [Just One Cookbook — Strawberry Matcha Latte](https://www.justonecookbook.com/strawberry-matcha-latte/) · [Japanese Taste — Strawberry Matcha Latte](https://int.japanesetaste.com/blogs/japanese-taste-recipes/how-to-make-a-strawberry-matcha-latte-using-j-taste-matcha-powder)

**Recipe (iced, layered):**
- Matcha base
- Strawberry purée: 120 g frozen strawberries + 2 tsp sugar + 1 Tbsp water, blended — makes 2 servings
- 180 ml milk
- 1 cup ice

**Steps:** spoon purée into the glass and level it · pile ice on top · pour milk slowly down the side · float matcha over the surface · serve with a straw.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 7 · energy 4 · fresh 7 · level 6
**Note:** Three colours in one glass and a jammy red undertow beneath the green.
**Caveat:** the purée is technically a sub-preparation, but it's one blender or one fork-mash — within the equipment ceiling. It also nudges N to 4, the ceiling.

### 8. Matcha Lemonade

**Provenance: modern, chain-popularised.** Widely documented as a Starbucks copycat; no Japanese lineage.
Source: [The Endless Meal — Matcha Lemonade](https://www.theendlessmeal.com/matcha-lemonade/) · [Rainbow Delicious — Honey Matcha Lemonade](https://rainbowdelicious.com/matcha-lemonade-recipe-sweetened-with-honey/)

**Recipe (iced, translucent):**
- Matcha base
- 30 ml fresh lemon juice
- 10–15 ml honey
- 150 ml cold water
- 1 cup ice

**Steps:** stir honey into lemon juice until dissolved · add cold water and ice · whisk matcha base · pour over and stir once · garnish with a lemon slice.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 0 · energy 5 · fresh 9 · level 4
**Note:** Lemonade that grew up and went to Kyoto.

### 9. Iced Matcha Coconut Water

**Provenance: modern.** A wellness-café drink; Sift & Simmer gestures at matcha's ceremonial heritage but makes no claim for this combination.
Source: [Sift & Simmer — Iced Matcha Coconut Water](https://www.siftandsimmer.com/iced-matcha-coconut-water/) · [Naoki Matcha — Matcha Coconut Water](https://naokimatcha.com/blogs/recipes/matcha-coconut-water)

**Recipe (iced, near-clear):**
- Matcha base
- 180 ml chilled coconut water
- Ice to fill

**Steps:** fill glass with ice · pour coconut water · float the hot matcha base on top · stir at the table.

**Axes:** coconut 8 · cream 1 · energy 5 · fresh 9 · level 3
**Note:** Electrolytes and chlorophyll — the drink you want at 3 pm in August.
**Menu note:** the lightest thing on the list, and the only one that's genuinely hydrating rather than rich. N = 2, the simplest recipe here.

### 10. Coconut Milk Matcha Latte

**Provenance: modern.** No traditional claim available.
Source: [A Pinch of Adventure — Coconut Matcha Latte](https://apinchofadventure.com/coconut-matcha-latte/) · [Matcha Konomi — Creamy Coconut Matcha Latte](https://matchakonomi.com/blogs/recipes/creamy-coconut-matcha-latte)

**Recipe (hot or iced):**
- Matcha base
- 180 ml full-fat coconut milk (canned, shaken — carton coconut drink is too thin)
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- ¼ tsp vanilla extract

**Steps:** warm coconut milk gently, do not boil · stir in maple and vanilla · froth · whisk matcha base · combine.

**Axes:** coconut 9 · cream 8 · energy 5 · fresh 3 · level 7
**Note:** Tropical and grassy at once, thick enough to coat the glass.

### 11. Hojicha–Matcha Layered Latte — ほうじ茶抹茶ラテ

**Provenance: modern format, traditional components.** Hojicha (roasted green tea) is an established Japanese tea; the two-tone layered latte is a contemporary café presentation. The layering works on density: sweetened milk sits heavy, the lighter tea floats.
Source: [Japanese Green Tea Co. — Layered Hojicha and Matcha Latte](https://www.japanesegreenteain.com/blogs/green-tea-and-health/layered-hojicha-and-matcha-latte) · [Hojicha Co. — Matcha & Hojicha Fusion Latte](https://hojicha.co/blogs/recipes/matcha-hojicha-latte) · [Just One Cookbook — Hojicha Latte](https://www.justonecookbook.com/hojicha-latte/)

**Recipe (iced; works hot):**
- Matcha base
- Hojicha shot: 2 tsp hojicha powder whisked with 60 ml water at 80 °C (or strongly steeped hojicha leaf, 1–2 min)
- 180 ml milk + 1 tsp sugar (the sugar is structural — it makes the milk heavy enough to hold the layers)
- ½ cup ice

**Steps:** pour hojicha shot into the glass · add ice · pour sweetened milk slowly · float matcha last · three bands, stir to a khaki swirl.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 6 · energy 5 · fresh 2 · level 6
**Note:** Toasted chestnut underneath, cut grass on top, milk holding the two apart.

---

### 12. Black Sesame Matcha Latte — 黒胡麻抹茶ラテ (Kurogoma)

**Provenance: modern drink, traditional ingredient.** Kurogoma is a wagashi staple; the latte is contemporary. Mochi Mommy notes the stirred colour turns "a soft minty blue-green" — the most unusual colour available to this menu.
Source: [Japanese Taste — Black Sesame Matcha Latte](https://int.japanesetaste.com/blogs/japanese-taste-blog/how-to-make-a-black-sesame-matcha-latte-using-j-taste-matcha-powder) · [Yunomi — Black Sesame Matcha Latte](https://yunomi.life/blogs/recipes/black-sesame-matcha-latte) · [Mochi Mommy — Black Sesame Matcha Latte](https://www.mochimommy.com/black-sesame-matcha-latte/) *(403 to automated fetch; cited from search summary)*

**Recipe (iced):**
- Matcha base
- 20 g black sesame paste (*neri goma*, Asian grocery) — or 1½ Tbsp toasted ground black sesame
- 1 Tbsp kuromitsu, or honey / brown sugar syrup
- 150 ml cold milk
- Ice

**Steps:** stir kuromitsu into sesame paste until loose · spoon into the glass and add ice · pour milk · float matcha · stir to a grey-green.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 8 · energy 4 · fresh 1 · level 8
**Note:** Halva and green tea, dark and sweet where every other drink is bright.
**Kuromitsu note:** Okinawan black-sugar syrup, sold ready-made or made from *kurozato* + water simmered 10 min ([Just One Cookbook — Kuromitsu](https://www.justonecookbook.com/kuromitsu-black-sugar-syrup/)). It is a drop-in sweetener upgrade for #2, #3, #12, #13 and #14.

### 13. Kinako Matcha Latte — きな粉抹茶ラテ

**Provenance: modern drink, traditional ingredient.** Kinako (roasted soybean flour) is the classic coating for warabimochi and mochi. Emeri gives no historical claim for the latte itself.
Source: [Emeri — Iced Matcha Kinako Latte](https://www.enjoyemeri.com/blogs/tea-room/iced-matcha-kinako-latte) · [Just One Cookbook — Kinako Milk](https://www.justonecookbook.com/kinako-milk/) · [Wikipedia — Kinako](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinako)

**Recipe (iced):**
- Matcha base + 1 tsp agave or honey stirred in
- Kinako paste: 2 tsp kinako + a pinch of salt + 1 Tbsp milk, mixed smooth
- 150 ml milk
- Ice

**Steps:** whisk kinako, salt and 1 Tbsp milk to a paste · spoon into an iced glass · pour milk slowly · pour matcha · stir once for a marbled finish.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 7 · energy 4 · fresh 2 · level 6
**Note:** Warm roasted soy under cold milk — like drinking a warabimochi.

---

### 14. Matcha Azuki Latte — 小倉抹茶ラテ (Ogura Matcha)

**Provenance: the *pairing* is genuinely traditional; the *latte* is modern.** Matcha + sweet azuki is a canonical Japanese combination — *uji kintoki* shaved ice, *matcha zenzai*, countless wagashi. Nio Teas frames the drink as translating that established pairing into a daily format, which is a fair characterisation.
Source: [Nio Teas — Matcha Red Bean Latte](https://nioteas.com/blogs/japanese-teas/matcha-red-bean) · [Just One Cookbook — Matcha Zenzai 抹茶ぜんざい](https://www.justonecookbook.com/matcha-zenzai/) · [Matcha Direct — Red Bean & Rich Matcha Two-Tone Latte](https://matchadirect.kyoto/blogs/matcha-recipes/red-bean-rich-matcha-two-tone-latte)

**Recipe (iced or hot):**
- Matcha base
- 3 Tbsp (~50 g) sweetened azuki paste (*tsubuan* / *ogura-an*, tinned at any Asian grocery)
- 150 ml milk, frothed
- Ice (if iced)

**Steps:** spoon azuki into the bottom of the glass · add ice · pour matcha slowly over the back of a spoon · top with frothed milk · stir from the bottom.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 7 · energy 4 · fresh 1 · level 8
**Note:** Whole beans at the bottom like sediment in a riverbed, sweet and mineral.

---

### 15. Matcha Affogato — 抹茶アフォガート

**Provenance: modern fusion.** The affogato format is Italian; Sugimoto (a Japanese green tea maker since 1946) calls their version "a green tea spin on the Italian classic." Honest fusion, not invented heritage.
Source: [Sugimoto Tea Company — Matcha Affogato](https://www.sugimotousa.com/blog/matcha-affogato-recipe) · [Nio Teas — Matcha Affogato](https://nioteas.com/blogs/japanese-teas/matcha-affogato)

**Recipe (hot poured over frozen):**
- Matcha base — made slightly longer, 3 g / 50 ml, so there's enough to flood the scoop
- 2 large scoops vanilla ice cream (or matcha ice cream for a double)

**Steps:** chill a small glass · drop in two scoops · whisk the matcha base hot · pour over the ice cream at the table · eat with a spoon, drink the dregs.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 10 · energy 7 · fresh 1 · level 9
**Note:** Bitter and hot hitting sweet and frozen — the best two seconds on the menu.
**Menu note:** N = 1, the shortest recipe, and the only spoon item. Temperature contrast is the whole point.

---

### 16. Matcha Sữa Đá (condensed milk)

**Provenance: modern, Vietnamese-derived.** Directly adapted from *cà phê sữa đá* by swapping matcha for phin coffee. Not Japanese in origin and I'd avoid implying otherwise on the menu.
Source: [Cooking Therapy — Matcha Vietnamese Iced Coffee](https://www.cooking-therapy.com/matcha-vietnamese-coffee-recipe/) · [Aiya — Matcha Vietnamese Iced Coffee](https://recipe.aiya-america.com/recipe/matcha-vietnamese-iced-coffee/)

**Recipe (iced):**
- Matcha base
- 30 ml sweetened condensed milk
- Ice to fill

**Steps:** spoon condensed milk into the glass · whisk the matcha base into it while still warm, until fully dissolved · fill with ice · top with 100 ml cold water or milk · stir hard.

**Axes:** coconut 0 · cream 9 · energy 6 · fresh 1 · level 9
**Note:** Sweet as fudge, with the matcha snapping back at the finish.

## Recommended final nine

Chosen to maximise spread across **colour, temperature, texture, and dairy content** — not nine lattes.

| Slot | Drink | Temp | Dairy | Colour | Why it earns the slot |
|------|-------|------|-------|--------|----------------------|
| 1 | **Usucha** | Hot | None | Opaque jade | The only genuinely traditional item; anchors every claim the menu makes |
| 2 | **Matcha Latte** | Hot | Dairy | Pale sage | The drink people arrive expecting; cutting it would read as contrarian |
| 3 | **Matcha Shakerato** | Iced | None | Jade + foam cap | Latte texture with no milk — a distinct *technique*, not a flavour swap |
| 4 | **Matcha Tonic** | Iced | None | Translucent + green float | Carries the sparkling and the clear/translucent requirements at once |
| 5 | **Ichigo Matcha Latte** | Iced | Dairy | Pink / white / green | Fruit-forward and the strongest visual on the menu |
| 6 | **Iced Matcha Coconut Water** | Iced | None | Near-clear | The lightest drink and the only high-coconut one; N=2 |
| 7 | **Hojicha–Matcha Layered** | Iced or hot | Dairy | Brown / white / green | Toasty register, plus the only three-band build |
| 8 | **Black Sesame Matcha Latte** | Iced | Dairy | Charcoal → mint-grey | Nutty and dark — the one colour nothing else on the menu occupies |
| 9 | **Matcha Affogato** | Hot on frozen | Frozen | White flooding green | Dessert-rich, spoon-eaten, and the only temperature-contrast drink |

**Coverage check:** hot ✓ (1, 2, 9) · clear/translucent ✓ (1, 4, 6) · sparkling ✓ (4) · fruit-forward ✓ (5) · nutty/toasty ✓ (7, 8) · dessert-rich ✓ (9).
**Dairy split:** 4 dairy-free, 4 dairy, 1 frozen dairy.
**Colour span:** jade, sage, foam-white, translucent, pink, near-clear, brown, grey-green, white-on-green.

### What was cut and why

| Cut | Reason |
|-----|--------|
| Iced Matcha Latte (#3) | Redundant against the hot latte + shakerato; the shakerato is the more interesting cold no-milk build |
| Yuzu Matcha Soda (#6) | Duplicates the tonic's sparkling/translucent slot. **Strongest reserve** — swap in if you want fruit *and* fizz in one glass |
| Matcha Lemonade (#8) | Third citrus-sparkling variant; weakest provenance (chain copycat) of the three |
| Coconut Milk Matcha Latte (#10) | Coconut axis already covered by #6, and it's a latte variation |
| Kinako Matcha Latte (#13) | Loses the nutty slot to black sesame on colour distinctiveness alone |
| Matcha Azuki Latte (#14) | The hardest cut — best traditional credentials after usucha. Loses the dessert slot to affogato only because affogato brings a temperature contrast nothing else has |
| Matcha Sữa Đá (#16) | Not Japanese in origin, and the affogato already owns "very sweet, very rich" |

**If you want ten:** add Matcha Azuki. It's the only cut item with real traditional grounding, and it fills a colour gap (deep red-brown) that nothing in the nine occupies.
